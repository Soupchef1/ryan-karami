Career Goals

The long-term vision

My big-picture goal is to work on advancing computing hardware. I want to be involved in the design process of frontier devices, whether they're for AI applications or memory. Ideally, I want to be a design engineer working at a more systems level, where I can have a lot of ownership over designs.
